Q: Which sport suits a 5-year-old?
A: Try fun, movement-first activities such as beginner soccer, swimming, gymnastics, or martial arts. A trial session helps reveal what they enjoy.

Q: Which sport suits an 8-year-old?
A: Soccer, basketball, swimming, tennis, cricket, and martial arts are all strong choices. Start with enjoyment, basic skills, and confidence.

Q: Which sport suits a 10-year-old?
A: At age 10, children can explore team sports, racquet sports, swimming, athletics, and martial arts. Let interest and regular attendance guide the choice.

Q: Which sport suits a teenager?
A: Teenagers can build around a preferred sport or try soccer, basketball, cricket, tennis, swimming, athletics, volleyball, or martial arts.

Q: How do I choose the right sport?
A: Consider your child’s age, interests, energy level, confidence, goals, and whether they enjoy team or individual activities. A trial session is a helpful first step.

Q: What sport is best for shy children?
A: Swimming, martial arts, tennis, and small-group skills sessions can help shy children build confidence at their own pace.

Q: What sport builds confidence?
A: Martial arts, swimming, soccer, basketball, and athletics can all build confidence through steady skill progress and encouragement.

Q: What sport improves teamwork?
A: Soccer, basketball, cricket, volleyball, and hockey teach communication, collaboration, and shared responsibility.

Q: What sport improves discipline?
A: Martial arts, swimming, tennis, and athletics reward focused practice, consistency, and personal responsibility.

Q: What sport improves fitness?
A: Swimming, soccer, basketball, running, cricket, cycling, and martial arts can improve overall fitness when practiced regularly.

Q: What sport improves coordination?
A: Tennis, basketball, soccer, cricket, gymnastics, and martial arts develop hand-eye coordination, balance, and body control.

Q: What sport improves stamina?
A: Swimming, soccer, basketball, athletics, cycling, and running are excellent for gradually building stamina.

Q: Can my child try more than one sport?
A: Yes. Trying different sports is a great way to discover interests. Keep the schedule manageable so your child still has time to rest and enjoy it.

Q: When should a child specialize in one sport?
A: Early childhood is usually best for broad movement skills and variety. Consider specialization later only when interest, enjoyment, and coaching guidance align.

Q: Is soccer good for beginners?
A: Yes. Beginner soccer builds movement, coordination, teamwork, and confidence. Comfortable sportswear, suitable shoes, and water are a good start.

Q: Is basketball good for beginners?
A: Yes. Basketball develops coordination, agility, teamwork, and decision-making. Beginners can start with basic dribbling, passing, and shooting skills.

Q: Is cricket good for beginners?
A: Yes. Cricket can develop focus, hand-eye coordination, teamwork, and patience. Coaches introduce batting, bowling, and fielding safely.

Q: Is swimming good for beginners?
A: Yes. Swimming supports confidence in water, fitness, coordination, and safety. Start with an age-appropriate, supervised class.

Q: Is tennis good for beginners?
A: Yes. Tennis develops balance, coordination, focus, and agility. Younger children can start with modified equipment and shorter courts.

Q: Is martial arts good for beginners?
A: Yes. Martial arts can build discipline, body awareness, confidence, respect, and focus in a structured environment.

Q: What equipment is needed for soccer?
A: Wear comfortable sports clothing, suitable soccer shoes, and shin guards if requested by the coach. Bring a water bottle.

Q: What equipment is needed for basketball?
A: Wear comfortable activewear and non-marking athletic shoes. Bring water; the academy will advise if you need your own ball.

Q: What equipment is needed for cricket?
A: Comfortable sportswear and athletic shoes are a good start. For batting, protective gear and equipment should follow coach guidance.

Q: What equipment is needed for swimming?
A: Bring swimwear, a towel, goggles if preferred, and a water bottle. Check with the academy about caps or other pool rules.

Q: What equipment is needed for tennis?
A: Wear comfortable activewear and suitable court shoes. Bring water; ask the coach whether a racquet is supplied for beginners.

Q: What equipment is needed for martial arts?
A: Wear comfortable clothing for the first session. The academy will advise when a uniform or protective equipment is needed.

Q: What should my child wear to training?

Q: Should my child bring water?

Q: Should my child bring a snack?
A: A light, healthy snack can be helpful before or after a session, depending on its time and duration. Avoid heavy meals immediately before activity.
