Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before your child starts their sports journey.

Which sport suits my child?

Consider their age, interests, confidence, and whether they prefer team or individual activities. A trial session is a great place to begin.

Can my child join with no experience?

Yes. Beginner programs introduce skills gradually while building confidence and enjoyment.

What equipment is needed?

Bring comfortable activewear, suitable athletic shoes, and a water bottle. Your coach will confirm any sport-specific equipment.

What are the academy timings?

Timings vary by sport and age group. Please send an inquiry for the current schedule.

What is the fee?

Fees vary by sport, age group, and program length. Contact us for current pricing.

How do I register?

Complete the contact form on the home page with your child’s details and preferred sport. Our team will follow up with next steps.

Is there a trial class?

Trial availability depends on the program. Contact the academy to learn about current options.

What happens in the first session?

Your child will be welcomed by the coach, complete a warm-up, try simple skills, and learn the session’s safety guidelines.

Can my child try more than one sport?

Yes. Exploring more than one sport can help children discover what they enjoy most.

How can I ask another question?

Use the Sports Compass AI Coach on the home page, or send us a message through the contact form.
